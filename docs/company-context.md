# Taxfix — company context (public facts only)

Source: public materials, summarised from the research folder in October 2026. Not official. No interview-derived content. Full research is outside the repo (path in CLAUDE.md); read it only when asked.

## Who they are
- Berlin fintech, founded 2016. One of Europe's largest digital tax-filing apps: 8M+ returns filed, about €5B refunded to customers.
- Roughly 370–400 employees; more than €530M raised. Not yet profitable; stated focus is profitable growth.
- Markets: Germany, Spain, UK under one Taxfix brand (TaxScouts was merged into it in 2025).

## What they sell
| Segment | Offer | Public pricing |
|---|---|---|
| Employees (consumer, DE) | Guided tax return, interview-style, no tax knowledge needed | Self-filing about €39.99 |
| Assisted filing | Expert Service | 20% of refund, minimum €99.99 |
| German self-employed (since 18 Mar 2026) | AI receipt categorisation, income/expense bookkeeping, VAT pre-filing (UStVA) via ELSTER, business registration | €19.99 net/month (first month free); annual return for non-VAT small businesses from €49.99 |

The self-employed product is their first recurring, year-round offer and first step into small-business customers. Named competitors in this space: Accountable, Sevdesk, Lexware, Kontist.

## How they present themselves
- Plain language, "no tax knowledge required", mobile-first.
- AI-first stance: public job titles such as "AI First Builder (Product / Design / Engineering)".
- Claim used in their self-employed marketing: about 75% of self-employed people see taxes as a burden, and over half want understandable automation.

## Why it matters for this prototype
- The prototype sits in their newest segment (German self-employed), where the product is young and the experience is still being shaped.
- Users are non-experts; trust and clarity matter more than feature depth.
- Submission goes through ELSTER (the German tax authority's system); the prototype does not connect to it.
