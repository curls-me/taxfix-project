# Persona: Nina, the self-employed consultant

**Status: draft.** Built from Marina's own description, not from user research. Items marked *(assumption)* or listed under Open questions are unconfirmed. Fictional person; no real data.

## Who she is

Nina is an EU citizen living in Germany. She is a freelance consultant who helps startups with go-to-market (GTM). She is very good at it, and her income depends on her time with clients.

- Smart, curious, excited by technology and AI.
- High standard for her own work, and expects the same from her service providers.
- Lifelong learner: happy to understand the German tax system better, as long as she is not drowned in detail.
- Has an "allergy" to German taxes: the system is complex and confusing to her.

## Today's solution (the bar we must beat)

Nina already uses Accountable. She is not a beginner looking for any tool: she is a satisfied-enough user who would switch only if the product is clearly better. Her switching cost is real (history, habits), and her bar is high because she expects the same quality from providers as from her own work. Details of what she likes and dislikes about Accountable are unknown *(to validate)*.

## What she cares about

- **Compliance and clean records.** Following the rules and keeping her books in order matters a lot to her.
- **Her time.** Every hour spent on admin is an hour not spent consulting and earning. Goal: spend as little time as possible getting documents into the app.
- **Privacy, completeness and correctness.** It is tax and finance, so she wants to know what happens to her data, and she wants nothing missing or wrong.
- **Understanding, in small doses.** She wants to know why something is the way it is, not read the law.

## How she wants to work

- Open to connecting tools (e.g. MCP) and doing admin through chat instead of forms and uploads.
- Wants the app to do the filing work and show its reasoning so she can check it.
- Will not trade correctness or privacy for convenience.

## Jobs to be done

Priority: **Core** = she cannot run her business without it. **Delighter** = not expected, but she would love it.

| #   | Job                         | Priority  | What it covers                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| --- | --------------------------- | --------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Register my self-employment | Core      | Register as self-employed and classify my activity correctly. The product serves both freelancers (Freiberufler) and trade businesses (Gewerbe), so Nina may be either; classification decides which registrations and taxes apply.                                                    |
| 2   | Invoice my clients          | Core      | Create, send and track quotes; convert accepted quotes into valid invoices and send them; record the resulting earnings and VAT.                                                                                                                                                       |
| 3   | Track my spending           | Core      | Know which expenses count as business and which receipts are valid; find them and get them into the system in an organised way; code them correctly. **Her biggest pain**, because receipts arrive from third parties in many forms.                                                   |
| 4   | Track my cash flow          | Core      | Make sure incoming and outgoing invoices are paid; match payments to invoices; see balances across my accounts. Understand how much tax returns she is expected to pay / receive ahead of the deadline so she can plan accordingly.<br>Delighter: also keep track of private spending. |
| 5   | Review and submit my taxes  | Core      | Understand which declarations are due and how often, and what goes into each; handle complex cases such as reverse charge and invoicing clients in the EU and outside the EU.                                                                                                          |
| 6   | Optimise my taxes           | Delighter | Save money legally: what extra expenses I can claim, whether a different legal form fits better, which exemptions apply.                                                                                                                                                               |

## Pains, by job

- **1:** Does not know which category her activity falls into, and a wrong choice has consequences later.
- **2:** Needs invoices to be legally valid and VAT correct without learning the rules.
- **3:** Receipts come as PDFs, emails, app screenshots, paper, from different suppliers; unclear which are deductible; chasing them is tedious. *(Her top pain.)*
- **4:** Payments and invoices live in different places and do not line up on their own.
- **5:** Does not know what is due when; cross-border cases (reverse charge, non-EU) feel risky to get wrong.
- **6:** Suspects she overpays but does not know where to look.
